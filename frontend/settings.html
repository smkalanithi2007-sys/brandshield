Data sharing and scan limits

BrandShield sends approved brand names to Brave Search and Apple Search. When an official domain is supplied, it sends the HTTPS homepage URL to Google Web Risk and requests the existing domain report from VirusTotal. BrandShield does not submit URLs to VirusTotal for scanning.

Search and catalog results are leads only. A match does not establish that an account, domain, or app is fake or unsafe. Review evidence from the source before taking action.

Automatic scans are opt-in and can incur provider charges. Manage each brand's schedule on the Monitoring page.

Account security

Passwords are hashed and sessions use HttpOnly cookies. Email verification, password reset, multi-factor authentication, and account deletion are not yet available; do not use a password you use elsewhere.
